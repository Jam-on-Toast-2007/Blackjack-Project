Card Counting

Sharpen the skill professional players use to find an edge at the table.

Card counting tracks the ratio of high cards to low cards left in the shoe so you know when the odds tilt toward the player. The Hi-Lo system assigns +1 to low cards (2-6), 0 to neutral cards (7-9), and -1 to high cards (10 through Ace). Add up every card's value as it's dealt to get your running count, then divide by the decks remaining to get the true count — the number that actually reflects your edge.
